### Contents:]

[web](/web)  contains a streamlit app with a search interface for the ratings data

[causality.ipynb](notebooks/causality.ipynb): a Jupyter notebook for processing vuln data and adding ratings generated by the CAUSALITY model. 

[HEAD](/HEAD) The latest ratings files available are kept here. If you're looking for a particualr model run output, check these

[2026](/2026) : CVEs from calendar 2026 that have been rated by the CAUSALITY model. There are multiple rating levels now ranging from fire to cold. This is being done with a multi-stage pipeline of models and algos and has nothing to do with severity, CVSs, EPSS, or the other conventional metrics. 

[2025](/2025) : CVEs from calendar 2025 that have been rated by the CAUSALITY model. There are two rating levels now, hot and warm.

[2024](/2024): Ratings runs for calendar 2024 CVES that came out of the model rated 'hot.' It is possible to predict most of the watch-listed CVEs will be in a subset of 6-12% of the population.
